Perfect Pointe Competitive Team

Technique. Performance.
Excellence.

Being part of our studio competition team is an exciting and rewarding experience. Three programs — one studio family — built on classical technique, real friendship, and the kind of training that shines onstage.

2024 & 2025 National Champions Act 1 Nationals
— 01 · The Big Team

The Competition
Team.

Being part of our Competition Team is both an exciting honor and a meaningful commitment.

Our Competition Team is where dedication meets growth. Dancers train at an elevated level with additional weekly classes and perform at multiple competitions each season, building strong technique, confidence, and lasting friendships.

We are extremely proud of our amazing dancers and talented teachers. We're proud to have earned the title of Act 1 National Champions in both 2024 and 2025.

Ballet Competition.

— 02 · For the future ballerina

By invitation only — a separate, prestigious track for our most dedicated classical dancers.

Ballet competition is its own world — and an essential one. It pushes our dancers toward the highest standard of classical technique, refines their artistry under pressure, and opens doors to scholarships, summer intensives, and conservatory training.

Our dancers compete at the most respected ballet competitions in the country: YAGP — the Youth America Grand Prix — alongside the prestigious Universal Ballet Competition and Ballet Championships of America. Many of our students have placed Top 24, Top 12, and Top 3 at YAGP, and we are proud to count multiple YAGP Finalists among our alumni.

— 03 · The Cutest Group of Dancers

Perfect Pointe Cuties.

Our younger, lower-commitment team — and truly one of the most special parts of our studio family.

Designed for Petite Pointers and Primary A dancers ages 4–6, this program offers an encouraging first step into the world of competition in a fun and supportive environment. Dancers perform together in one group routine, with optional opportunities to add solos, duets, or trios if desired.

Instead of 4–6 competitions, Cuties are only required to compete in 3–4 competitions.

PP Cuties & Big Team: Cuties dancers may also be part of our Big Team at this age, sharing the same competition schedule while competing at a lighter commitment level. Enrollment is through the Big Team, with the Cuties program added as an additional opportunity.
