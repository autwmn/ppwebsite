A Letter from the Studio

More than steps —
we teach young people
how to stand tall.

Perfect Pointe began in a small Covina studio with a simple but steadfast belief: that strong classical technique and joyful expression belong together. Our roots are grounded in the discipline and artistry of ballet — a foundation that continues to shape every dancer who walks through our doors.

Over the years, that vision has grown into a thriving community. Today, we offer comprehensive dance programs across all disciplines for students ages three through adult, welcoming both recreational and competitive dancers.

With a passionate and highly trained faculty, we are committed not only to developing strong, versatile dancers, but to mentoring confident, expressive individuals. Whether your child is here for an hour a week or twenty, our promise is the same: they will be seen, taught with care, and given room to grow.

Because what they gain here goes beyond technique: discipline, friendship, and a lasting belief in themselves.

Belle Maturo Founder & Artistic Director
Programs of Study

A curriculum
built on tradition.

Our programs follow the American Ballet Theatre National Training Curriculum — the same framework that trains professional dancers worldwide — adapted for every age, level, and ambition.

— 01

Petite Pointers

A 45-minute introduction to ballet for our youngest dancers. We focus on rhythm, expression, and the joy of music.

Ages 3 – 5
— 02

Classical Ballet

Pre-Primary through Level 5 of the ABT National Training Curriculum. Anatomy, alignment, and artistry — taught the way professional dancers learn it.

Ages 6+
— 03

Pointe

The moment every ballerina dreams of — dancing en pointe. Our program builds strength, precision, and confidence through safe, progressive training rooted in classical technique.

Ages 11+ · By placement
— 04

Jazz, Tap, & Hip-Hop

Energy, rhythm, and personality. Our commercial styles build versatile dancers who can hold their own in any audition room.

Ages 6+
— 05

Contemporary/Modern & Lyrical

Where technique meets storytelling. Classes that develop emotional range, musicality, and the freedom to move from the inside out.

Ages 6+

Adult Classes

Rediscover the joy of dance — at any age. Our adult ballet and tap classes offer a welcoming, energizing space to build technique, stay active, and move with confidence.
