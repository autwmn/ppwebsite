Payment

Billing & withdrawal
  • Annual registration is due at sign-up.
  • Tuition is due the 1st of each month; the late fee is applied on the 5th — no exceptions.
  • All accounts must keep a credit card on file. If payment isn't received by the 15th, your card is automatically charged, including the late fee.
  • To withdraw, notify us in writing by the 15th of the month prior. After the 15th, you're responsible for the following month's tuition.
  • If a dancer drops, misses a month, then returns, the registration fee must be paid again.
