Meet the Owner

Chloé Grové

Owner & Artistic Director

Ms. Chloé Grové brings to Perfect Pointe a career at the very highest levels of professional ballet — and a deep commitment to nurturing the next generation of dancers.

A Senior Soloist with Joburg Ballet (formerly the South African Ballet Theatre), Ms. Chloé performed numerous soloist and principal roles across a 16-year professional career. She was invited to perform with the Royal Swedish Ballet in Natalia Makarova's production of La Bayadère, and has shared the stage with celebrated international artists including Alina Cojocaru, Johann Kobborg, and Michaela DePrince.

Trained from age 4 in the RAD, Cecchetti, and Vaganova methods, Ms. Chloé worked with legendary choreographers and producers including Natalia Makarova, Maina Gielgud, Patricia Neary, and Ronald Hynd. She performed in all the great classics as well as neo-classical repertoire by George Balanchine, Kenneth MacMillan, and Choo San Goh.

An ABT® Certified Teacher, Ms. Chloé holds the Certificate in Ballet Teaching Studies (CBTS) with the Royal Academy of Dance and has completed the ABT® Teacher Training Intensive through Level 5 of the National Training Curriculum. She has coached numerous dancers for the prestigious Youth America Grand Prix.

As Owner and Artistic Director of Perfect Pointe, Ms. Chloé is honored to carry forward the exceptional foundation Belle Maturo built over 14 years — preserving the studio's reputation for world-class training and its warm, family-first culture while bringing her international professional experience to every class, every student, and every dancer's journey.

Our Founder

Belle Maturo

Founder

Belle Maturo founded Perfect Pointe School of Dance in 2010, building it into San Gabriel Valley's premier ballet and dance school from the ground up.

Over 14 years, Belle propelled the studio's growth and nurtured hundreds of students through her 30-year teaching career. She has been recognized with many dance and choreography accolades nationally and internationally, and holds numerous certifications in classical ballet.

Belle's greatest joy has always been offering the same quality dance instruction that brought her happiness growing up in Australia — creating a safe, encouraging environment that fosters an enduring love and passion for dance. That spirit remains the heart of Perfect Pointe today.
